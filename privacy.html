Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Overview

Publicar Toro is a personal tool used only by its owner, José Toro (yosoytoro), to upload his own videos to his own TikTok account through the official TikTok API.

2. Data the app accesses

After the owner authorizes it, TikTok provides an access token and basic account info (open ID, username, display name, posting privacy options), used only to publish the owner's videos and check upload status.

3. Storage

Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sent anywhere except TikTok's official API, and none is sold, shared or used for ads or analytics.

4. Other users

The app does not collect data from any other TikTok users.

5. Deleting data

Access can be revoked anytime in TikTok settings (Security > Manage app permissions) and the local token files deleted.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated; the latest version is always on this page.
